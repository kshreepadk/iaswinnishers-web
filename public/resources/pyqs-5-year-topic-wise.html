Polity

Prelims MCQ · 2025

Q. Consider the following statements about Raja Ram Mohan Roy: I. He possessed great love and respect for the traditional philosophical systems of the East. II. He desired his countrymen to accept the rational and scientific approach and the principle of human dignity and social equality of all men and women. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) I only  (b) II only  (c) Both I and II  (d) Neither I nor II

Answer: (c) Both I and II — Ram Mohan Roy respected Indian philosophical traditions while also promoting rationalism, scientific thinking, and social equality through reforms like the abolition of Sati.

Prelims MCQ · 2024

Q. As per Article 368 of the Constitution of India, the Parliament may amend any provision of the Constitution by way of:
1. Addition  2. Variation  3. Repeal
Select the correct answer using the code given below.
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3

Answer: (d) 1, 2 and 3 — Article 368 explicitly allows Parliament to amend any provision by addition, variation, or repeal, though not the Constitution's "basic structure."

Q. Statement I: The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) raised the policy repo rate cumulatively by 250 basis points between May 2022 and February 2023. Statement II: A tight monetary policy, achieved by raising interest rates, is used by the central bank to counter inflation. Which one of the following is correct in respect of the above statements?

Answer: Both statements are correct, and Statement II correctly explains Statement I — the rate hikes were a direct application of tight monetary policy to control inflation.

Prelims MCQ · 2024

Q. Consider the following: Pyroclastic debris · Ash and dust · Nitrogen compounds · Sulphur compounds. Which of the above are products of volcanic eruptions?

Answer: All four — pyroclastic debris, ash and dust, and both nitrogen and sulphur compounds, are typically released during explosive volcanic activity.

Environment & Ecology

Prelims MCQ · 2025 · Statement-based

Q. With reference to the planet Earth, consider the following statements: I. Rain forests produce more oxygen than that produced by oceans. II. Marine phytoplankton and photosynthetic bacteria produce about 50% of world's oxygen. III. Well-oxygenated surface water contains several folds higher oxygen than that in atmospheric air. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Answer: II only — oceans, not rainforests, produce more oxygen overall; marine phytoplankton and photosynthetic bacteria are collectively responsible for roughly half of the world's oxygen production.

Prelims MCQ · 2024/2025 · Species identification

Q. The organisms "Cicada, Froghopper and Pond Skater" belong to which of the following groups?

Answer: Insects — all three are insects (Class Insecta). Cicadas are known for their loud mating calls; froghoppers (spittlebugs) hold the record for the highest jump relative to body size of any animal; pond skaters walk on water using surface tension via hydrophobic legs.

Science & Technology

Prelims MCQ · 2024 · Statement-based

Q. With reference to Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generators (RTGs), consider the following statements: I. RTGs are miniature nuclear fission reactors used to power spacecraft. II. RTGs generate electricity from heat released by the natural radioactive decay of plutonium-238. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Answer: Statement II only — RTGs are not fission reactors ("nuclear batteries" is a more accurate description); they generate power purely from the heat of radioactive decay, most commonly of plutonium-238.
